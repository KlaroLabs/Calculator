This Privacy Policy explains how Klaro Labs ("we", "us", or "our") manages information in connection with the mobile application Klaro Calculator (the "App").

By downloading, installing, or using Klaro Calculator, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Information Collection and Use

Klaro Calculator is engineered for local device execution:

  • No Personal Identification: We do not require account registration, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or names to use our calculator.

4. App Permissions

Klaro Calculator requires minimal Android permissions strictly necessary for its functionality:

  • Internet Access (`android.permission.INTERNET`): Required solely to fetch currency exchange rate updates and serve Google AdMob advertisements.
  • Network State (`android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Used to check if an active internet connection is available before requesting ads or rate updates.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our services do not knowingly target or collect identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided any personal information, please contact us immediately and we will take prompt action.

6. Security

We value your trust. Because no personal user data or history leaves your device, your sensitive financial calculations and numbers are protected by the local sandbox security of your operating system.
